 My accessibility findings and fixes:

I noticed that my links had some contrast issues, especially when hovering over them. To fix this, I switched the colors so the background is brown and the text is yellow when hovering. When the links are not being hovered over, I changed the text color to dark blue and made it bold so it is easier to see and read.

I also changed the footer background from pink to brown because the pink was causing some contrast issues. These small changes helped make my website easier to read and improved its accessibility.
